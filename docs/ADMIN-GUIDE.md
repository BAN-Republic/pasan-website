# Updating your website

Everything on the site (films, showreel, bio, journey, awards, numbers, clients and photos) is edited in one place: the **admin page**. You never need to touch any code.

## Logging in
1. Go to **your website address followed by /admin** (for example yoursite.com/admin).
2. Sign in with the Google account or email you were invited with. If you have no invitation yet, ask your developer to add you as an **Editor**.
3. On the left you will see **Site settings**, **Films**, **Journey** and **BTS journal**.

## Publish and drafts
- While you type, your changes are saved as a **draft**. Drafts are private: visitors can't see them.
- Click the green **Publish** button (bottom right) to put your changes live. They appear on the site **within a few seconds**. Refresh the page to see them.
- Changed your mind? Use the menu next to Publish to discard the draft.

## Adding a new film
Open **Films**, click the **pencil/plus icon** at the top of the list, fill in the fields, then **Publish**.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Title | The film's name as it should appear on the site. Sinhala is fine. |
| Slug | Click **Generate**. It makes the film's own web link from the title. |
| YouTube link | Paste the video's link from YouTube (from the Share button or the address bar). |
| Client | The brand or company, e.g. Singer Sri Lanka. |
| Product | Optional, e.g. the product advertised. |
| Category | TVC, Promo film, Jingle · Music video, Documentary or Corporate. Visitors can filter by these. |
| Year | The year it was made. |
| Release date | Optional: the day it went up on YouTube. It helps Google show the video. |
| Roles | Tick what you did: Director, Cinematographer, Editor, Colorist, Producer. |
| Thumbnail | Optional. Leave empty to use the picture from YouTube, or upload your own still. |
| Description | Optional, up to 300 characters. Shown when the film is opened. |
| Featured | Turn on to make this the big card at the top of the portfolio. |
| Show on website | On by default. Turn off to hide the film. |

## Featured, hidden and order
- **Featured:** switch on **Featured** in the film. The first featured film in the list becomes the large card at the top.
- **Hide a film:** switch off **Show on website** and Publish. The film stays in the admin (marked "Hidden"), so you can bring it back any time. Nothing is deleted.
- **Reorder:** in the **Films** list, grab a film by the handle on its left and **drag** it up or down. The site follows this order. The same works for **Journey** and **BTS journal**.

## Changing the showreel
Open **Site settings** → **Profile** tab → paste the new link into **Showreel YouTube link** → Publish. You can also set **Showreel release date**.

## Bio, journey, awards, numbers and clients
All in **Site settings** except the journey:
- **Bio:** **About** tab → **Bio paragraphs**. One box per paragraph. **Quote** is the large quotation.
- **Awards:** **About** tab → **Awards**. Each award has a **title** and a one-line **detail**. To make a film name italic, select it and press Ctrl+I (Cmd+I on a Mac).
- **Numbers (stats):** **About** tab → **Stats**. Each one has a **value** (e.g. 18), an optional **suffix** (e.g. +) and a **label** (e.g. Years in film & TV). The numbers count up when visitors scroll to them.
- **Clients:** **About** tab → **Clients**. Type a name and press Enter to add it. Click the small × to remove one.
- **Contact details:** **Contact** tab → **Email**, **Phone** (write it with the country code, e.g. +94 77 123 4567, so it works for visitors abroad) and **Socials** (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Vimeo, LinkedIn; paste the full link to your profile). Leave a box empty to hide it.
- **Journey:** open **Journey** on the left. Each step has a **period** (e.g. 2012 – 21), a **title**, an **organisation**, a short **description** and a **status**: *Past*, *Present* (shows a red REC dot) or *Upcoming*. The text beside the Journey heading is **Journey intro** in Site settings → About.

## Behind-the-scenes photos
Open **BTS journal** → create a new frame → upload the **image** → write the **alt text** (a short description of the photo for blind visitors; required) → add a **caption** and a short **label** such as "Day 02" if you like → Publish. Drag frames in the list to change their order.

## Setting the focus point on a photo
On the website, photos are cropped differently on phones and computers. To keep the important part (usually your face) in view:
1. Click the photo in the admin, then the **crop icon** (or "Edit details").
2. Drag the **circle** onto the most important part of the picture. That is the focus point.
3. Optionally drag the **outer frame** to crop away edges you never want shown.
4. The previews underneath show how it will look at different shapes. Close the window, then Publish.

Every photo also has an **Alt text** box. Describe what's in it in a few words ("Pasan behind the camera on set"), because screen readers read it to blind visitors.
